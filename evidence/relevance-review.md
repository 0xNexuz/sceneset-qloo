# Small manual relevance review

Reviewed 2026-10-22. This is a qualitative inspection of deterministic fixtures, credentialed Qloo responses, and independently found operator/programme pages. It is a small set of positive controls and spot checks, **not** a representative ground-truth study or a measured accuracy rate.

| Query | Observed check | Human assessment | Remaining uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Afrobeats parties, Lagos/Accra/London | Generic London pubs and brunch places no longer qualify; Lagos venue with explicit Afrobeats party detail outranks them in the fixture. In the live run, Qloo returned six candidates per city; none of the 18 first-pass venue records mentioned Afrobeats and the focused retry returned zero places, so no city won. | Corrects the London-first false-positive pattern without inventing cultural evidence, but the live query was not actionable. Qloo resolved modern “Afrobeats” to its “Afro Beat” tag; these are related but not interchangeable genres, so the tag alone cannot establish the requested match. | A real Afrobeats-party listing and independently verified venue programme are still needed before claiming a positive result. [Fela Kuti's official Shrine history](https://felakuti.com/us/legacy/new-afrika-shrine) confirms a Lagos **Afrobeat** venue, not a specific modern Afrobeats party. |
| Food + live music | Pizza Hut food-only listing is rejected. Live Lagos/Accra sample gave one qualifying venue in each city; Accra won the tie. A three-city browser/API sample selected London, whose leading pubs had food and live-music metadata. [The Bedford's official pages](https://thebedford.com/livemusic/) describe live music and its [food menus](https://thebedford.com/menus/); [La Palm Royal Beach Hotel's page](https://www.gbhghana.net/web/dining/homowo) describes food and a live band at The Ghanaian Village Restaurant. | Two sampled venue operators independently support the combined venue fit; London here is a different, non-Afrobeats query. | Site descriptions do not verify a specific future show, table, schedule, or ticket availability. |
| Art + fashion | Gallery-only listing is rejected in the fixture. Live Lagos/London sample selected no city; Qloo resolved the query to “Antara Fashion Hall” but returned no venue satisfying both facets. | Conservative result, but the advertised suggestion is not currently useful. [Design Week Lagos's official FAQ](https://designweeklagos.com/faq/) describes a festival spanning fashion and exhibitions, with the National Theatre as its hub. That is evidence of a relevant *programme*, not evidence that a Qloo venue candidate passed both facets. | The provider does not return this as an evidenced place recommendation; individual programme dates and access still need checking. |
| Late-night jazz | Generic late bar is rejected in the fixture. Live Lagos/London sample resolved “Jazz” but selected no city. [Ronnie Scott's official visitor page](https://www.ronniescotts.co.uk/visit/plan-your-visit) identifies a London jazz club with late hours, while its [club page](https://www.ronniescotts.co.uk/about-ronnies/about-the-club) confirms live jazz. Ronnie Scott's was absent from the four London Qloo candidates in this run. | No unsupported jazz claim, but this positive control exposes a candidate-retrieval miss. | Published venue hours can vary; a dated performance and booking still need direct confirmation. |

This review deliberately separates **precision** (a returned venue actually fits) from **coverage** (the retrieval set includes known good venues). The two food/music spot checks support precision for those venues. Ronnie Scott's shows a coverage miss for jazz; the Shrine and Design Week examples show adjacent cultural/programme context but are not directly interchangeable with the tested Afrobeats-party/place queries. No accuracy percentage or general recall estimate is warranted.

Next evidence needed: obtain a licensed, date-aware event/programme source or expand Qloo candidate retrieval within the bounded request budget, then review a predeclared set of venue/query pairs across all cities. Do not use external pages to silently augment live Qloo results or imply event availability.
